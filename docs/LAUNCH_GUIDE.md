# Launch and growth guide

Code gets you a working game. Players and money come from how you present it, promote it and keep improving it.
This guide is a practical plan for that part.

## 1. Make it look like a hit before launch

1. **Icon (512x512).** One brainrot character, big, on a bright background. Sneaker Shark or Tralala Titan work well. No small text.
2. **Thumbnails (1920x1080).** Show a brainrot mid-jump over lava with a huge "STAGE 100" or "NEW BRAINROTS" headline. Three thumbnails is enough.
3. **Title.** Lead with what people search for: `Brainrot Obby [100 STAGES]`. Add an update tag later.
4. **Description.** Two lines about the game, then the active codes. Players search for codes.
5. **Character art.** The generated brainrots are good placeholders. Custom models (put them in `ReplicatedStorage > BrainrotModels` with the exact name) are the biggest visual upgrade you can make.
6. **Sounds.** Coin, checkpoint and death sounds make the game feel far better. Add them in `Config.Sounds`.

## 2. Launch week

- Post the promo codes in your description and on TikTok / YouTube Shorts. Short clips of ridiculous brainrot deaths do well.
- Use the free "Join group for +10% coins" reward to grow your group, and the daily streak to bring players back.
- A small Roblox sponsored-ads budget on day one helps the algorithm see your retention. Watch the results page and stop if the cost per play is bad.

## 3. Watch the numbers that matter

Open the experience's **Analytics** page on the Creator Hub.

- **Day-1 retention** should be above 20%. If it is lower, the early stages are too hard or the game is unclear. Lower world 1's `Difficulty` in `Config.Worlds`.
- **Average session time** should grow with updates. Brainrot unlocks at stage 30 and 60 exist for this.
- **Monetization funnel:** how many players open the shop, how many buy. Skips should be the top product. If not, make sure the SKIP STAGE button is visible on mobile.

## 4. Update rhythm

- Ship something every one or two weeks: a new world, 5 new brainrots, a holiday egg world. Adding stages is one entry in `Config.Worlds`.
- Put the update in the title and thumbnail, and post a new code with every update.

## 5. Ideas for later

- **Races:** a timed race mode with a prize for first place.
- **Badges** for stage 50, stage 100 and each prestige. They show on profiles and work as free advertising.
- **Private servers** for friends racing each other. Turn them on in the experience settings and set a price.
- **Seasonal worlds** with limited brainrots.

## 6. How the money works

Roblox pays developers in Robux. Robux earned from gamepasses and products appear in your group or account after a short hold, and you can cash out with the Developer Exchange program once you meet its requirements (a minimum balance and a verified account). Putting the experience under a group lets several people share earnings.

## 7. Be realistic

Most Roblox games get few players. The ones that take off usually launch, measure, and update every week for months. Treat the first version as a start, not a finish.
